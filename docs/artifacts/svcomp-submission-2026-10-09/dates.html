Community Discussions and Meetings

The preferred communication channel for general community discussion, topics specific to solving any technical problems, and questions is our Zulip stream. Please send an email to Dirk Beyer or Jan Strejček (competition organizers) for an invitation to the Zulip stream.

The joint SV-COMP/Test-Comp community meetings take place via this Zoom meeting room in the following dates. The meeting times alternate between 10:00 and 16:00 (CET), in order to make it easier for participants in different time zones. These meetings usually take up to one hour.

Important Dates

Oct. 12, 2026 Tool registration deadline (create merge request for <tool_id>.yml in FM-Tools repository)
Oct. 12, 2026 Submission deadline for verification tasks (via merge request in SV-Benchmarks repository)
Oct. 20, 2026 Tool submission deadline (all CI checks must succeed)
Nov. 3, 2026 Freezing of verification tasks (any pull request merged after this date invalidates the task)
Nov. 10, 2026 Notification of qualified tool
Nov. 17, 2026 Final tool resubmission deadline (the last chance to resubmit a new version of the tool, evaluation phase starts)
Dec. 1, 2026 Offline benchmarking phase completed and competition results communicated to the participants (evaluation phase ends)
Dec. 4, 2026 Publication of competition results
Dec. 17, 2026 Submission of competition-contribution papers
Jan. 14, 2027 Notification of competition-contribution papers
Jan. 21, 2027 Camera-ready submission of accepted competition-contribution papers
